What do the orbit terms mean?
orbits every …
How long the moon takes to travel once around its planet — the moon's "year."
prograde / retrograde
Prograde moons circle the planet the same way it spins. Retrograde moons go the opposite way — usually a sign the moon was captured rather than born alongside the planet.
regular / irregular
Regular moons ride near-circular orbits close to the planet's equator and most likely formed with it. Irregular moons follow tilted, stretched, faraway orbits and are usually captured asteroids or comets.
tilt
How steeply the orbit is angled relative to the planet's equator (0° lies flat in the equatorial plane).
e — eccentricity
How stretched the orbit is: 0 is a perfect circle; closer to 1 is a long, narrow oval.
